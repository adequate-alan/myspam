## 2. Product rules that must not be undone

1. **The base SPAM Board is never overwritten by derived calculations.** League-adjusted values, custom formats, Sleeper data, stats refreshes and projections are separate layers computed in the browser. They must never write back into `RANKINGS_CSV` or change rank/tier/value for anyone.
2. **Manual rankings are locked.** Players with `source=manual` keep the order, tiers and values Alan set. Automatic processes (projections, Auto players, the weekly job) must never reorder them or push them down.
3. **Auto players sit underneath.** `source=auto` players (70 added Oct 2, 2026) are ranked below every manual player (overall #184+), valued with a gentle tail that never exceeds anyone ranked above them. Moving, re-tiering or re-valuing an Auto player in the editor and saving makes him Manual. Players who only shift because someone else moved stay Auto.
4. **Typed values set the board's level** (Alan, Oct 5; `calibrateToAnchors`): each typed value is compared with the model value of his slot, and that ratio bends the model curve smoothly (interpolated between typed players by rank; above the first, its ratio with #1 capped at 10,000; below the last, it fades to 1 over `VALUE_MODEL.anchorFade` = 25 ranks), so players next to a typed value sit just around it instead of a cliff (Oct 5: Chase was 16% under Lamar's 7,786). `applyTiers` no longer rescales the board so the top model player is 10,000 (that made every value depend on who shared RB tier 1 with Gibbs). A far-off typed value lifts or lowers its whole stretch, so keep them in line (the editor warns).
5. **Values follow the overall rank (decided by Alan, Oct 3, 2026).** On the SPAM Board no player is ever valued above someone ranked ahead of him. `keepOverallOrder` enforces it over the whole board (Auto tail included) by adjusting values, never ranks. **No two players share a value** (Alan, Oct 4; 94 players were tied, e.g. six at 2,263 and nine at 1,021 around Croskey-Merritt's published value): each model value is at least `VALUE_MODEL.minRankStep` (0.25%) below the one ranked ahead, found as the closest fit to the tier values (pool-adjacent-violators on log(value + 200) − i·log(1 − step), so a tied run spreads both ways instead of sinking); values under 400 at the bottom step by whole points; only players at 0 can tie. Published values are fixed anchors: the model values between two of them stay strictly between them, with a smaller step if the stretch is too crowded. (This replaced the 2% `overallMaxShift` cap and then a pooled 0.5% step that still left ties.)
5. **Tiers first, then rank order, then format.** Tiers decide where the value gaps are; rank order inside a tier sets small gaps; format (league size, scoring, lineup) adjusts how much each tier is worth. No artificial cliffs just because a player crosses QB12→QB13 or WR36→WR37.
6. **"Custom" means unpublished.** A typed value shows the CUSTOM label, striped bar and an Auto/Reset button only until it's published. Once published it's that player's official SPAM value and looks like every other player. It stays fixed (it doesn't follow the model) until someone edits it again.
7. **Save → Publish workflow.** Before saving = unsaved edit. Save changes = saved (in the browser, and published automatically if the browser has a GitHub token). Publish to live site = committed to `index.html` on GitHub and becomes the master rankings. Tier changes follow the same workflow.
8. **League-specific screens always show league-adjusted values and position ranks** (Trade Finder, My Team, League, Free Agents, the calculator in league mode, player pages opened from those). The SPAM Board / League-adjusted toggle only affects the Rankings board. Never show the base positional rank (e.g. superflex QB3) inside a 1QB league's trade recommendation.
9. **Historical honesty in Trade History.** Every trade gets two separate AM verdicts, never mixed (Alan, Oct 8): **at the time** (process: was it fair when it happened?) and **today** (outcome: how it aged). "At the time" uses each player's latest published Ranking History entry at or before the trade timestamp (never an update from after it, not even the same day) and exists only when every valued player had one. Trades before AM existed are labeled **Pre-AM trade** and say "No AM value existed at the time of this trade"; partial history says so too. Never present today's values as what a player was worth back then.
10. **Stats never change rankings.** Stats refreshes update game logs and fantasy points only.
11. **Design consistency.** Use the theme tokens; no default browser controls (selects, checkboxes, dialogs). No Patreon button or styling. Labels are sentence-case in copy, uppercase with letter spacing for small labels.
12. **The website is the master source of truth for rankings** (from Alan). Don't sync the Excel file unless Alan asks.
13. **Keep the layers separate** (from Alan). Don't overwrite manual rankings or published values, don't let stats or projection updates change rankings, and keep league calculations derived from the base board.
14. **Stay in scope** (from Alan). Don't change unrelated parts of the site when doing a specific feature. Preserve the SPAM branding and design system.
15. **Don't rename the `spm_` localStorage keys** (from Alan). Renaming them would wipe visitors' saved leagues, edits and tokens.
16. **League data is private to each visitor** (from Alan, Oct 4). The League hub is a template that works for any Sleeper league: everything in it (username, selected league, rosters, standings, matchups, transactions, waiver activity) is fetched in the visitor's browser and kept there (`spm_sleeper` in localStorage; matchups only in memory). Never commit anyone's league data, never hard-code a league, team or manager, and never make a personal league a public dataset. Tests use synthetic leagues only. Public/shared: base SPAM rankings, player identity, stats, ranking history, design, trade-value and league-adjustment logic.

## 3. How to run it

It's a static site: **one HTML file** plus JSON data. No build step, no framework, no npm.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/adequate-alan/myspam.git && cd myspam
python3 -m http.server 8000          # then open http://localhost:8000/
```

Opening `index.html` straight from disk works for most things, but `fetch()` of `data/*.json` needs a local server.

Pipeline scripts (Python 3.12, pandas, numpy): `cd pipeline && pip install pandas numpy`. They download public nflverse / DynastyProcess data.

## 5. How the rankings data flows

1. **`RANKINGS_CSV`** (inside `index.html`) is the master data. Columns:
   `player,pos,team,rank,pos_rank,tier,value,proj_ppg,games,proj_score,sleeper_id,source`
   (`tier_name` is not in the current header; the editor's `colOf()` adds it as a 13th column the first time a tier is renamed.)
   - `rank` = overall rank (source of truth for order); `pos_rank` derived from it.
   - `tier` = positional tier number; **90+ are tag tiers** (e.g. 90 = "Hurt"), not part of the ladder.
   - `value` = blank → model value; a number → published official value (fixed).
   - `source` = `manual` / `auto`. `tier_name` = editable tier name (falls back to `TIER_NAMES`).
   - `proj_ppg/games/proj_score` = written weekly by the pipeline (projection blend). `sleeper_id` written by the pipeline.
2. On load, `loadPlayers` builds player objects and values (base SPAM values, scaled so #1 = 10,000).
3. Editor edits change a `draft` copy of the CSV and call `rebuild()`. Save stores the changed fields per player in `localStorage` (`spm_local_edits`); Publish merges only the edited fields onto the latest `index.html` from GitHub and commits it (so the weekly projection refresh is never clobbered).
4. The **league layer** is computed after every rebuild from the base values: never stored.

## 7. How to change rankings safely

- Prefer the website editor. If editing `RANKINGS_CSV` by hand:
  - Keep the columns.
  - Keep ranks unique and contiguous.
  - Put every Auto player after every Manual player.
  - Quote any field containing a comma.
  - Leave `proj_*` and `sleeper_id` alone (pipeline-owned).
- After resolving any `RANKINGS_CSV` merge or rebase conflict, check that ranks still run 1–N with no duplicates or gaps and that every Auto player is below every Manual player. Verify by parsing the CSV, not by eye.

## 9. Sleeper and league data are separate from SPAM data

- Connected leagues (Sleeper username → leagues; ESPN direct or paste; Yahoo/other paste) are stored in the **visitor's browser** (`spm_sleeper`). Nothing about a visitor's league is committed to the repo.
- Every import becomes a Sleeper-shaped object `{league, users, rosters}`, so all features work the same way.
- Ownership: `LG.ownerOf` maps players to fantasy teams for owner tags, the "Show" filter, Free Agents, rosters.
- Trade History reads Sleeper `transactions` for each week (Sleeper only).
- **League-adjusted values** come from the selected league's settings (Synced league) or the user's **Custom format** (`FMT`, saved per browser, also in `?fmt=`). League size + lineup set replacement levels; scoring re-scores the points-by-rank curves; tiers are re-priced as blocks; `tierKeep` limits tier-to-tier drops in shallow formats; a small `floor` (25, easing down by position rank) stops a format from pushing values to ~0, but never lifts a player above his base value (so a base-format league equals the base board exactly; a player at 0 on the board shows 0 everywhere); a per-player scoring-profile nudge (max ±10%) applies; within a position, order moves only when a value beats the player above by >3% (max 2 spots), and a value is capped just below the player ahead at his position (published values too); **across positions the overall league-adjusted order follows value** (`mergeByValue`: the position queues merged by adjusted value, ties to the earlier board slot), and the Auto tail is capped below the lowest manual player, so **no lower value is ever ranked ahead of a higher one** (Alan, Oct 4: Tyler Warren 3,281 sat ahead of DeVonta Smith 3,367 in a custom 12-team 1QB format because the old 3% margin also applied across positions; `tests/format_check.py` now checks four formats for inversions). In the base format nothing moves. **None of this touches the SPAM Board.**
- **TE premium swing is left to the replacement math** (Alan, Oct 5): removing TE premium cuts elite TEs ~27–29% and mid TEs ~40% (TE1's edge over replacement falls from 9.2 to 6.7 PPG: the bonus is worth 3.2 PPG to him but only ~0.7 net to replacement, and fewer TEs win flex spots). Considered damping it toward market-style +15–25%; Alan's call: in non-TE-premium leagues TEs really are worth less, so no damping.
- **FLEX spots are shared, not all-or-nothing** (Alan, Oct 8, after the TE valuation audit; claude.ai artifact "AM TE Value Audit"; `replLevels`, `FORMAT_ADJUST.flexSoftness` = 1.0 PPG). The old rule gave every league FLEX spot to whichever position's next starter scored most. With TE premium four of the twelve went to TEs (TE replacement TE17; TE21 at +1.0, TE25 at +1.5); without it none did (TE13). The TE+ base board carried that deeper replacement, so no-TEP leagues stripped mid-TE value twice: TE8–TE12 fell to ~WR43 in 12-team 1QB (≈0.81× FantasyCalc, 0.67× in Superflex), with a −45% TE7 → TE8 cliff, and no TEP → +0.5 more than doubled them (+113%). Now each FLEX / W-R / W-T spot is split by a softmax over the eligible positions' next-starter PPG (a position 1 PPG behind still gets ~27%); Superflex spots keep the all-or-nothing rule (they go to QBs); starter counts can be fractional and the curves are read in between. Effects in 12-team 1QB Full PPR no TEP: TE1 6,767 → 7,214, TE5 2,240 → 2,491, TE8 1,071 → 1,308, TE16 264 → 342; TE7–12 ≈ 1.0× market; TE8's TEP steps +74% / +50% / +28%; TE1 now rises with league size 8 < 10 < 12 < 14; RB/WR −2% in no-TEP leagues, TEs −10% at TE +1.0; the base format is unchanged. Not done (Alan's call): mid TE1s still read ~WR41 in 3-WR leagues (WR27 with 2 WR + FLEX; market WR36–39), mostly from the board's TE tier 3 → 4 break (Juwan Johnson #62 → Loveland #71, −29%); moving TE8–TE12 is a ranking decision. Rejected: replacement as a band average (WR +17% at TE +1.0), rank-by-rank format pricing (broke deep QBs), a flat TE multiplier. `SPM.formatCheck()` checks TE8's TEP steps shrink (0 → +0.5 under +90%), TE1 rises with league size, and TE7–12 ≥ 0.85× / TE13–20 ≤ 1.1× FantasyCalc (12-team 1QB Full PPR, no TEP; skipped without the market file).
- League depth (`DEPTH_ADJUST`): shallow leagues (e.g. 10 teams × 7 starters) weight bench depth less.
- **Custom format over a connected league must always be visible** (Alan, Oct 4). A Custom format replaces the league's own settings for every value (`valueLeague()`), so: the league chip shows "Custom · <format>" in gold, and a notice bar on every tab says which format the values use instead of the league's, with **Use <league>'s settings** (`useLeagueSettings`). Connecting or switching to a different league goes back to that league's own settings; a refresh of the same league keeps the custom format. (The Oct 4 "Superflex QBs collapsed" report was a saved 1QB custom format silently overriding a Superflex league; the Superflex math was correct and unchanged.)
- **1QB QB replacement** (Alan, Oct 5): in 1QB leagues the QB replacement level sits `FORMAT_ADJUST.qb1Cushion` (0.5) of the way from the last starting QB down to the waiver QB (`modelTable`), since teams carry and stream QBs. 12-team 1QB QB5–QB12 went from ~25% to ~35% of their Superflex values (Allen 3,714 → 4,472); QB13+ stay low. Superflex/2QB are untouched. Raising the cushion further barely moves QB5–12 because the QB tier is priced as one block.
- **8-team superflex QB smoothing** (Alan, Oct 6; `FORMAT_ADJUST.sfStarterTeams` = 8, `sfStarterEase` = 0.75, in `adjustValues`): pricing tiers as blocks put cliffs inside the starting QBs of an 8-team superflex/2QB league (about 16 QBs start, yet QB4 Mahomes 4,475 → QB5 Burrow 3,146 and QB12 Goff 2,866 → QB13 Stafford 1,146). In superflex/2QB leagues of up to 8 teams, while QBs are weekly starters (teams × (QB + superflex slots)) no QB falls further below the QB ahead than on the SPAM Board; after the last starter each eases down at most 75% of the board's step per spot until he reaches his format value. It only raises values, never above the base value. 8-team SF now: Allen 5,999 · Mahomes 4,475 · Burrow 4,364 · Goff 3,974 · Stafford 2,975 · Love (QB16) 2,710 · Nix 1,467 · Cousins 1,095 · Herbert 633. 1QB leagues, 10+ team superflex (10-team SF still has the QB12 → QB13 cliff, 3,807 → 2,084; raise `sfStarterTeams` to include it) and the base format are unchanged.
- **Superflex QB starter floor, larger leagues** (Alan, Oct 6; in `adjustValues` after `smoothTiers`): in superflex/2QB leagues over `sfStarterTeams` teams, a QB who is still a weekly starter (rank ≤ teams × QB slots) keeps at least what his own rank is worth in that format priced rank by rank (board value × the format's model at his rank ÷ the base model there); only raises, never above his board value or the QB ahead. Came from the Oct 6 value audit (376 mock trades; 10-team SF was the worst format) and a check against 2023–25 QB finishes: 10-team SF QB5–8, QB13–15 and QB19–20 rise (Burrow 4,171 → 4,634, Stafford 2,029 → 2,300, Herbert 553 → 758, Darnold 550 → 633); average miss vs history 4.4 → 3.7 points of QB1's value; no other position or format changes. Extending the 8-team smoothing to 10 teams was tried first and rejected: it overshot history for QB9–18 (QB12 at 60% of QB1 vs 35%). Still open: QB18 → QB19 is ~45% in 10-team SF (history ~16%) because the SPAM Board itself has a tier break there (Cousins #60 → Herbert #81, QB19 ≈ 21% of #1), a ranking call (§11 board-vs-history note). Other audit findings not acted on yet: formats widen tier gaps far beyond the board's (Goff → Stafford 40% on the board, 150% in 1QB), and the package adjustment undervalues real starters added to a deal (Chase for Higgins + Hall: equal raw values, Chase's side +34%).
- Superflex/2QB are applied through replacement levels: `replLevels` fills each SUPER_FLEX slot with the position whose next starter scores most (QBs in practice), so a 12-team Superflex league starts ~24 QBs and QB replacement drops accordingly; there is no separate league-size penalty before it. `SPM.formatCheck()` guards it (Allen, Jackson, Purdy and QB20 must be ≥1.4× their 1QB values in 12- and 10-team leagues; QB20 ≥3×; QB12 2QB ≥1.4× 1QB) and reports when a custom format overrides the selected league.

  - **Buckets** (Market aligned · Slight / Buy / Strong, buy or sell): the smaller of a rank level (log of (rank + 3) ratio: 0.18 / 0.35 / 0.6) and a value level (AM value gap 250 / 600 / 1,200), +1 when the consensus spot sits 2+ AM tiers away with a 400+ gap; Strong needs a tier break and both sources; under 300 on both sides = noise. **Confidence** High / Moderate / Low from the size, FantasyCalc–Flock agreement, freshness, format match and whether AM's spot is manual. Big gaps on manual players get **Strong AM conviction** (you moved his rank or tier in the last 21 days) or **Ranking deserves review**.
  - **Market Leverage in Trade Finder (Alan, Oct 8, refined the same day; `ML_MAX`, `mlAssess`, `mlTypes`, `mlWhyYou`, `mlWhyThem`, `mlCard`):** find trades where AM says you win while the external market says the other manager wins or gets fair value; sell what the market likes more than AM, buy what AM likes more than the market. Never changes rankings or values; a market disagreement is never treated as a ranking mistake. Three things are judged separately and never blended into one verdict:
    - **Asset value (AM Edge):** how much AM prefers your side (league-adjusted values, package adjustment). Search range 2–30% (`ML_MAX`): a computation boundary, not a realism rule; a big AM Edge ranks *better* when the other signals hold up (score 0.25 · min(1, edge/20)). Bands: 2–5 Small, 5–10 Good, 10–20 Strong, 20–30 Very Strong; +1 when the Market Edge for them is ≥ 8%, −1 when it's under −3% or Market Confidence is Low. Labels "Small / Good / Strong / Very Strong Leverage" (on the card "… asset leverage").
    - **Market Edge:** how much the FantasyCalc/Flock consensus prefers their side (same package adjustment, on AM's scale). An acceptance signal, not a rule: allowed down to −8%; below −3% only with a real roster gain for them (market-weighted roster net ≥ +2% or a weak room fixed).
    - **My Roster Impact:** your best starting lineup before → after, as a % (`myLine`; power rank movement is shown only as context, since ranks can be tightly packed). A ranking penalty in steps, never a tight cutoff (Alan: "penalize, don't immediately eliminate"): 0% or better none, 0–3% minor (−0.03), 3–6% meaningful (−0.08), 6–10% strong (−0.16), 10%+ severe (−0.28). Only extreme damage is dropped: −20% or worse, or −15% or worse for under 5% AM Edge (a +4% asset gain for a −14.5% lineup stays, ranked low). A 2.5% hard gate was tried the same day and removed.
    - **Acceptance Plausibility (High / Moderate / Low):** only about the other manager, never your roster or the size of your AM Edge: 35% market view, 30% their roster benefit in market terms (each incoming piece weighted by what it means to their roster, so filler counts little), 20% positional need (weak room fixed > room improved > a new starter), 15% construction (starters vs filler, losing their best player), −0.15 for opening a hole. Capped at Moderate when their lineup drops > 3%, a room falls into the bottom third, or nothing they get would start; Low when their lineup drops > 5% or they lose their best player for less than two starters at a market loss. Hard gates: they need a real reason (a new starter, a better room or lineup), their lineup can't drop > 6%, no starter out for bench pieces, their best player only for a real upgrade, market-weighted roster net ≥ −12%, and filler never bridges an elite gap (their best outgoing piece needs a piece back worth ≥ 55% of it, or two starters).
    - **Ranking:** 0.25 AM Edge · 0.15 Market Edge · 0.15 my roster (fit + lineup) · 0.10 their roster · 0.30 Acceptance · 0.05 simplicity, minus the lineup penalty. The package adjustment and best-player effect in `tradeModel` are unchanged (Alan asked to keep them).
    - **Card (`mlCard`):** partner, leverage pill, trade size · You receive / You send (each player with AM and Market position ranks, starts-at notes, "on IR") · **AM** Receive · Send · AM Edge and **Market** They receive · They send · Market Edge for them · "Strong asset leverage · Short-term lineup downgrade"-style line · My starting lineup ±% · PR #a → #b and Their starting lineup ±% · type tags (`mlTypes`: Immediate Upgrade, Asset Value Gain, Depth Gain, Consolidation, High-Upside Bet) · Acceptance Plausibility and Market Confidence pills · **Why you do it** / **Why they may do it** (slot upgrades like "Upgrades their RB2 from RB31 to RB16", surplus depth into a starter, one elite asset for two starters, the market valuing what they get above AM; never "values are close") · both teams' power and room moves; Full analysis adds a per-player AM vs Market / FantasyCalc / Flock / Market confidence table and the sources' format notes. Works in Shop my players, Target a player and Any team. Shop flags read "Market Sell Opportunity"; Find targets has **Best Market Targets** ("Strong Target" / "Target", AM +N) and **Best Market Sells** (Market +N, Market Confidence).

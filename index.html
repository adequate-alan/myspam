              Publish to the live site

              Publishing writes your edits to the repository on GitHub, so it needs a GitHub access token for an account that can write to it. You only do this once per browser.

              Create one at github.com → Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Tokens (classic) with the repo scope (collaborators on this repo need a classic token). Signed in as the repo's owner, a fine-grained token for this repository with Contents and Actions: Read and write also works. It stays saved in this browser until you sign out.

              Connect Sleeper

              Enter your Sleeper username to import your leagues. SPAM only reads public league data from Sleeper, so no password is needed.

              Choose a league

                League settings

                Pick your league's format and the rankings, values and trade calculator adjust to it. At each position the SPAM order stays put unless a player's value changes enough to pass someone; across positions the board follows the adjusted values, so nobody is ranked ahead of a higher value.

                Or bring in your league:

                Connect your league

                Bring in your league's settings and rosters for league-adjusted values, My Team, power rankings, free agents and the Trade Finder. Nothing is posted to your league.

                Just want different scoring?

                Connect ESPN

                Paste your league page's address or just the league ID (the number after leagueId=).

                ESPN didn't let this site read the league directly. That's normal for private leagues. Copy it from your own browser instead:

                1. Open your league's data on ESPN in a tab where you're signed in to ESPN.
                2. Select everything on that page (Ctrl+A, or ⌘A on a Mac) and copy it. In Firefox, click Raw Data first.
                3. Paste it here.

                Connect Yahoo

                1 · League settings

                On Yahoo, open League → Settings, select everything (Ctrl+A or ⌘A), copy, and paste it here. Skip it to use the settings from League settings.

                2 · Rosters

                Open each team's roster page, select everything, copy, and paste it into its own box. Start with your team and the teams you trade with; you can add the rest later.

# Adequate site audit: Impeccable audit + critique, Oct 12, 2026

Method: dual-agent critique (Assessment A design review and Assessment B detector and browser evidence ran as two isolated sub-agents), plus the `/impeccable audit` technical pass. Impeccable 4.5 skill with engine 0.1.14, installed at the user level for this session (`npx impeccable install --providers=claude --global -y`). The skill, the CLI detector and the in-page `detect.js` bundle all ran. Nothing generated by the installer is committed.

## What was inspected, and the limits

- **Rendered pass on the latest main** (merge of PR #18, hover cards, and PR #17, market freshness): 160 screens. That is 20 sections, both themes, at 1440, 1024, 390 and 375 px.
  - Sections: Rankings before and after a league is connected, the WR tab, hover and tap cards, keyboard focus, six drawer tabs, My Team, all six League tabs, Trade Finder, and the Trade Calculator loaded from a past trade.
  - Data: the synthetic 12-team league from `tests/league_hub_check.py` on the frozen Week 4 clock, so no real league data was used.
- **Computed-style scan on every screen**: page overflow, elements past the right edge, text under 11 px, targets under 24 px, text contrast against its computed background, native `title` count and truncated text.
- **The live site itself could not be opened.** The container's network policy refuses `adequate-alan.github.io`, the Adobe Fonts kit, Sleeper's photo CDN and ESPN's logo CDN.
  - The audit ran on the same commit that deploys (main's head; its deploy run was in progress at the time).
  - Screenshots show the Arial fallback instead of Proxima Nova, initials instead of headshots, and abbreviations instead of team logos. No finding below depends on those assets. Where a real asset would change a judgment, the finding says so.

Design specificity verdict: **authored, not interchangeable.** Signals: the position badge system, the wordmark, the ▲/▼ movement and tier notation, the 59 px player row, the Ranking History step chart, and copy like "Approximate rebuild · 1 check to look at" and "Pre-AM trade". The weakness is cohesion: each tab speaks its own dialect of badges, tabs and headers. The detector agrees: no template tells, and its findings are either deliberate density choices or the contrast issues below.

## Findings, ranked

Severity: P0 blocks a task. P1 is a major difficulty or a WCAG AA failure. P2 is an annoyance. P3 is polish.

### Fixed in this PR

1. **[P0] Phone, no league: Connect league was off screen.** At 375 px the header row was 504 px wide, so the onboarding button sat at x = 389 with no way to reach it.
   - Cause: `.mast-right` is `flex: none`, so it never wrapped.
   - Fix: phones lay the header out as logo + theme, then League settings + Connect league side by side, then Last updated. The league-connected header is unchanged.
2. **[P0] Phone rankings board: Value cut off.**
   - No league (the default landing, editor on): the table kept a 610 px minimum, so Position and Value were off a 341 px box. They were reachable only by an unmarked sideways scroll.
   - League connected at 375 px: the table was 358 px in 341, so values read "10,05" and the header "VALU".
   - Cause: the owner line ("OWNED BY MANAGER11", letter-spaced, no wrap) and the name + team pair (no break opportunity) set the player column's minimum width.
   - Fix (≤560 px):
     - The owner line truncates and drops its "Owned by" prefix (the full name stays in the tooltip).
     - The team mark can drop under a long name.
     - The editor columns lose the 610 px minimum and use tighter padding.
     - Photos are 30 px under 400 px.
   - Result: table width equals the container at 375, 390 and 430, in both modes.
3. **[P1] Focus ring under 3:1.** The site-wide `:focus-visible` color was `--focus-line` (#4A4E57 on #08090B ≈ 2.3:1 dark; #9699A1 on #ECEEF2 ≈ 2.5:1 light), which fails WCAG 1.4.11.
   - Fix: a `--focus-ring` token (#A7ABB3 dark, #50545C light, both ≥ 6:1), used by the global ring, the hover-card cells and the editor bar.
4. **[P1] Status text unreadable in dark mode.** "OUT" on My Team, Rosters, Waiver Wire and Matchups used `--st-out` #93202A as text, about 2.2:1. "DNP", "Tough", "Drop" and the loss badge used #C9373F, 3.7:1.
   - These colors are fills; as text they need a lighter shade.
   - Fix: `--st-out-text` #E8706A and `--st-dnp-text` #E2625D in dark mode, ≥ 4.9:1 on every dark surface. Light mode keeps its colors, which already pass. Badges keep the deep fills.
5. **[P1] "My team" in brand red at 3.9:1.** The small caps line on the board, the "You" tags, the rail name and Power Rankings were affected.
   - Fix: `--mine-text` (#FF3D5E dark, #C80027 light, ≥ 4.8:1). The red edge, dot and fills keep `--mine-accent` #E4002B.
6. **[P1] Brand red leaking into ordinary statistics and structure.** This contradicts the §10 rule that red is for selection, your team and CTAs. Each item now uses neutral or semantic color, with red kept for your own team:
   - Power Rankings team-score bars were all red (`--gold`, which became red on Oct 9). They are now neutral, and your team's bar stays red.
   - The Matchups share bar's left half was red on every matchup. Both halves are now neutral, and only your side is red.
   - Standings division bands were tinted red. They are now a neutral raised surface.
   - My Team's Starters / Bench / IR bands were tinted red with red rules. They are now neutral.
   - The League page eyebrow ("ALPHA LEAGUE · 12-TEAM …") is now muted.
   - The light-mode theme toggle (moon icon) was red. It is now neutral, like the dark-mode sun.
   - "On waivers · clears Wed" on the Waiver Wire is now amber (`--warn`), the status color for pending.
7. **[P2] Muted text dimmed below AA by extra transparency.**
   - The footer: 3.0:1 light, 3.5:1 dark.
   - The board's "Tier 1" tags: 4.0:1 dark.
   - Both now use `--muted` straight (≥ 4.9:1). This is the Oct 11 rule that muted text stays at 4.5:1.
8. **[P2] Light RB green as text at 4.4:1.**
   - It covers ~15 element types: ▲ movement, "Rising", "Good" matchup, the W result, Live, High confidence and others.
   - Fix: `--pos-rb` light #0F8A55 → #0C7F4E (5.05:1 on white). Visually the same emerald.
9. **[P2] Drawer header: "High confidence" ran into "NEXT 4"**, and "Tough stretch" wrapped to an orphan line (1440 drawer).
   - Fix: Next 4 gets the same "·" separator as the other figures, and its Tough / Soft stretch tag now stays inside the Next 4 item.
10. **[P2] Waiver Wire and My Team on phones: the NFL team floated at the far right** of a wrapped name ("Brock / Bowers ……… LV"). It now follows the name.
11. **[P3] League settings chip label sat high in its 36 px box.** It is now vertically centered.

### Recommended, needs Alan's call (not changed)

1. **[P1] The editor bar for every visitor.** It is 40 px on desktop and three lines on phones, and reads "Drag or use the arrows…" for visitors whose edits stay in their own browser. It also drives the phone editing layout, which now fits but is crowded.
   - Option: show the bar and the drag column only after sign-in, or after a visitor's first edit.
   - Product decision: Steven's temporary "editing open to all".
2. **[P1] One control vocabulary.**
   - Segmented switches: AM Board / League-adjusted and By division / League are full size, while the calculator's AM Value / Roster Impact is a ~60% copy.
   - Badges: filled position badges, outlined slot badges with a colored edge, and rounded transaction pills, where WAIVER CLAIM's blue reads as WR.
   - Page headers: Rankings and the Calculator have none; League has eyebrow + title + subtitle; Finder has title + subtitle.
   - Naming: "Waiver Wire" is both a League tab and a Transactions filter.
   - Proposal: one switch size, one badge primitive with variants by meaning, one page-header pattern.
3. **[P2] Trade Calculator verdict color.** "Team 2 is getting robbed" renders in side-A brand red at about 30 px. Side colors are a system (A red, B coral), but the headline reads as an alarm.
   - Option: neutral headline, with side colors kept in the side chips and the bar.
4. **[P2] Drawer header hierarchy.** About 12 numbers sit before the first tab, in four label/number pairings, with no single focal number.
   - Option: ROS PPG as the one large figure, then Season / Healthy / AM / FPTS / PPG / Value as one muted row.
5. **[P2] ▲/▼ means two things.** Under the rank it shows movement since the last update on the AM Board, and the difference from the board in League-adjusted view.
   - Option: a different glyph or a column-guide label in League-adjusted view.
6. **[P2] Rankings at 1440: ~380 px of empty space between "Tier 1" and the value.** The value sits far from its badge, and the thin meter under it reads as an underline.
   - Option: cap the Position column and pull Value inward, or use the space for a weekly projection or PPG column.
7. **[P2] The Waiver Wire renders every available player** (~250 rows, 7,800 px), with no position filter above the fold.
   - Option: paging like the Trade Finder ("Show 25 more").
8. **[P3] Matchups density.** Cards are ~215–260 px with wide side margins on desktop.
   - Option: an optional compact one-line-per-matchup view.
9. **[P3] Phone navigation mixes two patterns.** The main tabs scroll sideways, while the League sub-tabs wrap to two rows.
10. **[P3] Data text under 10 px.** "Auto" (8.96 px), the Recent form week labels (9 px) and the Matchups bar label (9 px) are data, not the documented small uppercase labels.
    - Option: a 10 px floor for data text.
11. **[P3] Brand red as small text elsewhere.** `--gold-bright` #F01B43 on raised dark surfaces is 4.0:1, and the "Show lineups" link is 3.9:1. Hover states of red buttons are 3.8–4.2:1.
    - Option: route red text through the new `--mine-text`-style shade.
12. **[P3] Name suffixes wrap alone on phones** ("Kenneth Walker / III"). A non-breaking space before Jr. / II / III would fix it. Not done here because several tests compare names by text.

The in-page detector (`detect.js` injected into the rendered page) found 551 / 290 findings on Rankings at 1440 / 390, almost all per-row repeats of the same small-label rules. Its real contrast cases were the footer, the dark tier tags, the light League settings label (4.3:1) and the light RB green. The first two and the last are fixed here; the League settings label stays marginal.

## What works (keep)

- **The board row:** name, team, owner line, filled position badge, muted tier, bold tabular value and a thin position meter. Dense without cramping, with semantic color used correctly.
- **Rosters:** slot, weekly state, the season rank dot vs the AM rank chip, GP / FPTS / PPG and the schedule rail. The densest, most finished screen.
- **Honesty affordances:** "Partial projection · 1 starter missing", "Approximate rebuild", "Provider update unknown", "Pre-AM trade". Rare, and they build trust.

## Verification

- Every screen was re-rendered after the change: no page errors, no page overflow.
- The phone board's table width equals its container at 375, 390 and 430 px, in both modes.
- Contrast failures in the scan went from 1,552 to 1,090 instances. The remainder is dominated by false positives on translucent badge fills and the P3 red-text cases above. One new failure appeared and is a false positive: the light RB badge, composited on a translucent fill.
- Clipped text stayed at 212 instances before and after. Owner tags on the phone board now truncate with an ellipsis instead of widening the table.
- Trade-off: on phones the owner line reads "MANAGER11" without the "Owned by" prefix. "My team" and "Free agent" are unchanged, and the full text stays in the tooltip.
- Test suites: the full local run (`python3 tests/run_ci.py full --jobs 3`) passed all 28 suites with no new failures. The only non-passing lines are the documented advisory checks: two value-curve board preferences and one drag timing check.
